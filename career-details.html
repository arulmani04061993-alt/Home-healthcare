Care Assistant

Full Time 9 AM - 5 PM Brooklyn, NY
APPLY NOW

Job Description

We are seeking a compassionate, dedicated Care Assistant to join our team. In this role, you will be responsible for providing non-medical, in-home support to seniors and individuals needing assistance with daily living activities. You will play a crucial role in improving the quality of life for our clients by ensuring they are safe, comfortable, and well-cared for.

Job Responsibilities

  • Provide companionship and emotional support to clients.
  • Assist with personal care, including bathing, dressing, and grooming.
  • Perform light housekeeping tasks, such as laundry, dishwashing, and tidying up.
  • Plan and prepare nutritious meals according to dietary requirements.
  • Remind clients to take their prescribed medications.
  • Accompany clients to appointments and social outings.
  • Document daily activities and report any changes in the client's condition.

Qualifications

  • High school diploma or equivalent.
  • Previous experience as a caregiver or in a similar role is preferred but not required (training provided).
  • Valid driver's license and reliable transportation.
  • CPR and First Aid certification (or willingness to obtain).
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Patience, empathy, and a genuine desire to help others.

Other Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay with weekend differentials.
  • Comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance.
  • Paid time off (PTO) and sick leave.
  • Ongoing paid training and career development opportunities.
  • Supportive, team-oriented work environment.
